The Story Behind the Wall

Where the City's Heartlines Come Alive

Walk past our bright saffron-yellow wall, look up at the twinkling canopy of fairy lights, and you’ll find Karachi’s soul right here.

Painted with love right on our courtyard facade, you can trace the silhouette of Mazar-e-Quaid, the iconic banking towers, Frere Hall, and the gentle waves of the Arabian Sea dotted with traditional sailing boats.

My Karachi Café is where the old-school charm of roadside chai khanas meets a refined, aesthetic open-air aesthetic. Whether you are unwinding after midnight with college buddies, finishing client work over a hazelnut cappuccino, or catching up over crispy parathas, there is always a warm wooden chair waiting for you.
